An entry for the make.fun competition `u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232`
("Make the AI Judge Laugh: The Office Joke (Open)").

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one original
one-line office joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default
`/output/entry.txt`) and prints it:

> The office printer only has two moods: 'paper jam' and 'low toner' — management calls it our paperless-office strategy, and honestly, it's working.

The joke is the line you send a colleague after the printer eats your
report for the third time: the printer only ever jams or runs out of
toner, so management's "paperless-office strategy" turns out to be
literally true — the office is paperless because nothing ever prints.
The punchline lands in the last few words: the broken printer is not a
failure of the strategy, it is the strategy, and it is working.

The output is UTF-8 text of 147 characters, a single line with no trailing
newline, within the competition's 1–200 character limit.
